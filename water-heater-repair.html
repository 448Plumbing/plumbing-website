When is it time to replace instead of repair?

Most tank-style water heaters last 8–12 years. If yours is in that range and showing signs of rust, leaks, or frequent breakdowns, replacement may be the safer, more cost-effective option. Tankless systems can last longer but still require maintenance and occasional repairs.

Water heater repair FAQ

How long does a typical water heater last?

Traditional tank-style heaters usually last 8–12 years, depending on water quality and maintenance. Tankless systems can last longer with regular flushing and service.

Should I repair or replace my water heater?

If your unit is relatively new and the issue is minor, repair often makes sense. If it's older, severely rusted, or leaking from the tank itself, replacement is usually the safer option. We'll walk you through each choice with clear pricing.

Do you repair tankless water heaters?

Yes. We service many major tankless brands and can help with error codes, ignition problems, and performance issues. We also provide annual maintenance like descaling and filter cleaning.

Can I prevent future water heater issues?

Regular maintenance like flushing sediment, checking anode rods, and verifying safety valves can extend the life of your heater. We can set up a simple maintenance schedule for your home.
